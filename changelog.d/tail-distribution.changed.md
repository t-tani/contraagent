Whether a task can finish before a deadline is decided from the samples of its kind and tail key, no longer from their median: a task is late when fewer than half of its samples (its own wall time and the work after it) fit in the time left, and each queue pass asks again as tasks finish. `JournalEstimates` keeps the 100 most recent samples of each kind and tail key and gives the share that fits (`JournalEstimates.finish_chance`); `WorkQueue(finish_chance=)` and `Run.queue(finish_chance=)` take it, a `FinishChance`; `Run.queue` gives the run's own when it also uses the run's own estimates. The queue's order still uses the median.
