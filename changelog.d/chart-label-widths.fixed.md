The charts of `@contraagent/elements/charts` measure their axis labels in the font they draw them in, so a label in a wide font, such as DejaVu Sans, no longer runs past the left edge of a heatmap or a state grid. The helper `textWidth`, an estimate from the number of characters, is removed from `@contraagent/elements/charts`. On the documentation site's landing page, the replay's band of numbers grows when a wide font wraps a tile's label, instead of pushing the window's content past its edge.
