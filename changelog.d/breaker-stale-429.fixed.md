A 429 moves a failover chain's breaker only when it comes from the hop in force, or from the probe on the primary. The 429s of the other launches that ran on the primary when its limit came each counted as a failed probe, so five of them put the first probe two hours away, not fifteen minutes. A 429 from a hop the breaker had already left moved it back, recorded a switch that did not happen and started the cool-off again. A 429 on a hop that one task reached on its own walk, after another stop, moved every task past that hop
