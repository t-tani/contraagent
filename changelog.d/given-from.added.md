`plan.jsonl` records where each task's params came from: `given_from`, the keys of the tasks whose outputs had reached the flow call that created the task (from its start, through its parent, and from the flow calls whose end it awaited: `FlowDriver.received`) and are among the task's params: a value whose JSON is that of a whole output, or of one whole row of an output that is a list (`Origin.given_from`, monitor `PlanNode.given_from`). A copy of an output counts as the output; a field taken out of it does not, and created from still shows that the task came after it. It is shown only, never part of the fingerprint or of what a resume skips; a resume's divergence is judged by it. The monitor draws a task after the tasks it was given from, as it drew a task after the tasks it read, and counts them among its earlier tasks (`TaskIO.upstream`) and their later ones (`downstream`).
