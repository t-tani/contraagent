`plan.jsonl` records where each task's params came from: `given_from`, the keys of the tasks whose outputs the flow had received and whose content, or a part of it, is among the task's params (`Origin.given_from`, monitor `PlanNode.given_from`). A copy of an output, or a field of it, counts as the output. It is shown only, never part of the fingerprint or of what a resume skips; a resume's divergence is judged by it.
