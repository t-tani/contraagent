The elements of `@contraagent/ui` and `@contraagent/elements` fit the box they are given, at any width, not the window's: their layouts follow container queries on their hosts. In a narrow `<ca-monitor>` the header wraps and the run button shortens its run line, and the tab list scrolls sideways with a shade at each edge that has more tabs past it; `<ca-definitions>` puts its list of kinds above the definition, and a file's "Show source" goes under its name; wide tables scroll inside their own box. The model names of a selected session tab in `<cae-transcript>` take the secondary text colour, which reads at 4.5:1 on the selection's tint.
