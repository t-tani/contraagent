`contraagent diagnose RUN_DIR` says what a run's records show to change in its pipeline, kind by kind, and launches nothing: the checks that sent outputs back (a validator by its name, a field of the output model, the file itself), the launches stopped at a limit of their budget, the tool calls the permissions refused, a safety classifier's refusals, the routes passed over, the attempts that ended in another stop, and the tasks that cost several times the kind's median. Each names its tasks, the transcript of the first, and the change it suggests with the topic that explains it. With `--against EARLIER_RUN_DIR` it also says what changed since an earlier run of the same tasks, such as one the run resumes from: the parts of the fingerprints that changed, the tasks that pass now and did not then and the reverse, and the outputs that differ. The new topic `contraagent docs improving` gives the steps.
