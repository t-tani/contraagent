The monitor and the elements follow more of what a reader sets. With no theme chosen the monitor follows the system's colour scheme (`system`, where it was contraagent Dark). The sizes of text are in rem, so the font size set in the browser reaches them, and the page asks for the operating system's text scale where the browser offers it. In forced colours (Windows High Contrast) a status mark, a bar, the selected tab and a selected option keep their shape, drawn with the system's colours and with lines. `prefers-contrast: more` gives stronger lines and no muted text in every theme. A reader who asks for less motion still sees a colour fade to its next value. A scrollbar's thumb reads at 3:1 and no longer changes under the pointer. A run's view is the main landmark of the page, the page's title names the tab and the run, a task's views are a named tab list with the arrow keys, and a press on a button answers at once. While the page is hidden the clocks draw nothing and the run list is not asked for; a code block colours its text once, not at each render; two fingers on the graph zoom the page.
