A launch on omp 19 keeps to its task as on omp 18. omp 19 reads the skills it learned from the user's own sessions (`omp-managed`) and the hosts of the SSH config (`ssh-config`) through two new providers, which the launch's config now turns off with the others; it also turns off omp's memories and its auto-learn, so a task's session neither reads what omp remembers of the user's sessions nor teaches it anything. The monitor shows the files an omp 19 edit changes, which omp gives in the edit's text (`[path#tag]`) and not as its `path`. A task's `Edit(path)` and `Write(path)` hold on such an edit, as before.
