**A transcript that grows reads as a calm log.** `<ca-transcript>` given a longer `.events` that starts with the same events as the last one appends the new events instead of drawing the whole transcript again: the rows already drawn stay, and the scroll position is kept. `<cae-transcript>` follows new content with a smooth scroll (at once with reduced motion), keeps following while that scroll is under way, and fades in once when another section takes the place of the one shown.
