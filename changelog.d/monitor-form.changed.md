The monitor has a new form, in which the layout says what a value is and a colour is a status, the accent or an identity. Its header is one line: the name, the run, the connection and one button for the settings, which hold the theme, the language and the list of keys (they were a theme menu, a language switch, a clock and a line of key hints). The tab list has no icons, a count is a number after its name, and the filter of the tasks and the history stands at the end of the tabs' line. The overview opens on the state of the run as its headline, with the problems and the outputs side by side under it; the totals are a row of figures with lines between them, and the graph stands in its panel without a frame of its own. In the tasks list a task's status stands beside its name, and a run of one day shows the times without the day. A task's detail shows its transcript as text on the page; a tool call that names a path keeps the file name when the line is short, the rest about the task stands beside the views without a box, each attempt is a line, and the agent and the model are shown without their labels. The history narrower than its columns gives up the agent and the tokens and puts a reason under its row, where it scrolled sideways. The surfaces of contraagent Dark and contraagent Light are one neutral hue in even steps of lightness, with no gradient and no shadow under a panel; text is 12px at least; corners are 4, 6 and 10px, and a pill is a status or a count.
