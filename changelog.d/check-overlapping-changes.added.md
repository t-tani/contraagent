`contraagent check` warns of two tasks that may run at once in one working directory and whose `changes` patterns may cover the same files (the same pattern with no `{unit}`, or a directory above another's): a failed attempt of one would remove the files the other created meanwhile. Tasks one after the other (`after=`), tasks that share a claim one task holds at a time, and tasks of a kind that runs one at a time are not warned of
