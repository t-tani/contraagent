A launch that the operating system refuses for its own command is not launched again: arguments and environment too long (`E2BIG`), an executable it cannot run (`ENOEXEC`) or may not run (`EACCES`). The launch fails with `launch_failed`, its reason says that the command is refused on every launch, and its result is not retryable (`LaunchResult.retryable`, `TaskResult.retryable`): neither the kind's `retries` nor the queue's retry of a launch that never ran tries it again. The task still fails over to the next route, which has its own command. A missing executable (`ENOENT`) and every other launch failure are retried as before. `Backend.launch_failed` takes `retryable=`.
