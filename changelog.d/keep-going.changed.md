The documentation states the failure rule that runs already follow (keep going): a failed task stops only the code that awaits its output and the flow calls that await that code; every other flow call and every queued or running task goes on, a parent that ends leaves its children running, and `asyncio.TaskGroup` is the choice for all or nothing. The examples that fan out give each unit a flow call of its own and gather them with `return_exceptions=True`: the demo's and the review example's `docs`, the analysis example's `analyse` and `analyse_bundle`, the tutorial's `triage_and_plan`, and the `missing` and `rows` examples' `reviews` report on the units that got through; `implementations`' `review_all` reviews every unit at once and leaves a failed one out of its counts. The review example adds `publish`, all or nothing with a `TaskGroup`.
